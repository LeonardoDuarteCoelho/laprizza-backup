LaPrizza Backups

Privacy policy

Updated: 19 November 2026. This notice describes the Google Drive backup integration being configured by the LaPrizza academic project team.

Information accessed and purpose

The integration requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It accesses backup files and their metadata created by the integration to upload, locate, verify, download and remove backup copies. Google Drive access is used for project backup and recovery operations.

Database backup archives may contain personal information held by the project, including user and customer records. The integration's planned transfer procedure encrypts archive content and file names with rclone crypt before storing them in Google Drive.

Storage and access

Remote copies are stored in the project team's Google Drive account. Authorized backup operators maintain the rclone configuration, OAuth tokens and recovery secrets in restricted storage. Local backup files require their own access protection.

The public information pages do not request OAuth access or receive backups, credentials or tokens. They contain no application analytics scripts or advertising.

Use and sharing

The team uses the files and metadata accessed through this integration to operate and validate backups and recovery. It does not sell this information, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Google provides the Drive storage service; access to the backup account and recovery secrets is restricted to authorized operators.

Retention and recovery

Operators must configure and document retention as part of SYS08 and verify its enforcement. Requests affecting personal data, including completed erasure or access restrictions, must be reconciled before restored information returns to normal use. Retention and restore controls are still being implemented.

Revoking access

The Google account owner can revoke this integration's access in the account's third-party connections settings. Revocation stops future authorized operations; it does not itself delete backup files already stored in Drive. Contact the project team to coordinate file deletion or other data requests.
