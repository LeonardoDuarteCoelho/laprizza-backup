Terms of use

Updated: 19 November 2026.

Authorized use

This integration is intended for authorized backup operators of the LaPrizza academic project. Operators must have permission to process the source data and use the destination Google Drive account.

Operator responsibilities

Operators must protect account access, OAuth credentials and recovery secrets, use the encrypted backup destination, monitor storage and transfer results, apply the documented retention policy and test recovery. Recovery secrets must remain available outside the backed-up server.

Readiness and service dependencies

The integration is currently under configuration. Routine operation requires completed and tested backup, retention and restore procedures. A successful file upload alone does not demonstrate database consistency or recovery objectives.

Operation depends on Google Drive availability, account permissions, storage capacity and valid OAuth authorization. Operators are responsible for monitoring these dependencies and responding to failed backups.
